Frequently Asked Questions

Is MentorLinks free to download and use?

Yes! The app is 100% free to download. Many peer tutors volunteer their time at ₱0.00 / hour to earn University Community Service Hours. Mentors who charge set their own rates, and students pay them directly.

How does the Direct Pay method work?

Unlike commercial platforms that take 20–30% cuts, MentorLinks connects students directly to mentors. You pay the mentor directly using GCash, Maya, or in-person cash, and input the payment reference number in the app for verification.

How do mentors accredit University Community Service Hours?

When a volunteer session is marked completed, our database triggers automatically commit verified hours to the mentor's profile. Mentors can generate and export official Service Hour Summaries directly from their Profile tab.

Why does Android warn that the file might be harmful?
